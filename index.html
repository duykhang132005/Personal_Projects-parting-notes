How to play

Build

  • Pick a singer (hotkeys 1–5), click again to cancel.
  • Place on open ground beside the path.
  • Press Start wave or Space.

Controls

  • Space — start next wave; during a wave, toggle speed.
  • Esc — pause / resume.
  • S — sell selected singer (50% refund).

Upgrades

  • Click a placed singer for targeting and two upgrade paths.
  • Crosspath max is 3/2 (or 2/3).

Notes

  • Jazz — glittery; heavy hits get capped.
  • Folk — needs big damage or the hit does nothing.
  • Each Alto adds gold on kills.
  • Closing Night — conductor Andy Clark cues and stuns the choir.

Lawn Approach · first call

Warm-up tips

1. Pick Soprano — tap the Soprano seat in the shop (hotkey 1). She hits one note at a time; buy Damage upgrades to unlock chain echoes.

2. Place — click open grass beside the path. Ghost range shows where she’ll sing.

3. Start wave — when the lawn is set, press Start wave. Notes march toward the hall.

4. Targeting & upgrades — click a placed singer for targeting and two upgrade paths (crosspath max 3/2; labels like Breath / Tone). Open Help anytime for full controls. Later stages tip a hat to Abendlied, Glorious Apollo, and closing-night cues.
